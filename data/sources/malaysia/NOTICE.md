# Sources for the synthetic Malaysian sample store

The file `data/kedai_amber_synthetic.csv` is **synthetic**. Orders, customers, quantities,
prices, discounts, costs, profits and returns are generated by `scripts/generate-malaysia-store.ts`.
No row describes a real sale, shop or person.

Two official open datasets shape it. Both are published by the Government of Malaysia under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

| File here | Source | How it is used | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lookup_item.csv` | PriceCatcher item lookup, KPDN and Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM), via [data.gov.my](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/lookup_item) | Product names, pack sizes, groups and categories | Copied as published on 3 Nov 2026. The generator drops cooked food, title-cases names and adds English group and category labels |
| `iowrt_3d_471_472.csv` | Index of Wholesale & Retail Trade by 3-digit MSIC group, DOSM, via [OpenDOSM](https://open.dosm.gov.my/data-catalogue/iowrt_3d) | Month-to-month shape of retail sales (groups 471 and 472) | Filtered to the absolute series for groups 471 and 472. Only the within-year shape is used, never the RM levels |

Branch locations use real state and district names from the PriceCatcher premise lookup
([data.gov.my](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/lookup_premise), CC BY 4.0). Branch names are
invented ("Kedai Amber"), and no branch corresponds to a real premise.

© Department of Statistics Malaysia. The source data is used with modification, and no
endorsement by KPDN or DOSM is implied.

**Assumptions made by the generator, not taken from any source:** prices and margins by
category, a 2% yearly price drift, how strongly each category's units respond to discounts (written to
`data/kedai_amber_price_truth.json`), weekday and payday effects, festival dates and uplifts (Chinese
New Year, Hari Raya Aidilfitri, Hari Raya Haji, Deepavali, Christmas) entered by hand for
2023–2025, 11.11 and 12.12 online sale days, basket sizes, planted product pairs, member purchase
rates and drop-off, the share of walk-in shoppers, and return rates. Check the festival dates
against the official gazette before relying on them.
